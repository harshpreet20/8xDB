What this record does not support

Facts, not a verdict — asserting more than this carries invites an easy rebuttal.

  1. Malicious intent is not evidenced. The record proves 12 days of non-supply then 20 images within 8 hours of escalation — consistent with concealment, equally consistent with an unmanaged vendor. Deficiency in service is provable on the failure itself; you don't need intent. What is documented: the "we cannot access the factory" line was made 3m35s after the vendor sent a video of the finished goods — plead that as a contradiction, not as proof of motive.
  2. Phone-call content is not evidence. The exports log call duration only, no transcripts. Either drop call content or write a dated, signed recollection now (date, duration from the log, what was said).
  3. Some delay is attributable to RCC. Sizes given in cm against an inches system (flagged 15 Jul 17:00); 3 sizes unresolved through 16 Jul 13:58; 2 pieces added 22 Jul 08:21 (after the 20 Jul window, used to justify the 27 Jul reset); logo-error ownership accepted by client 29 Jul 17:28. Concede these first in writing — it removes the vendor's rebuttal.
  4. Colour is the weakest item, not the strongest — screen-approved, perceptually contestable. Lead with ribbed collar/cuffs instead: agreed 5 times, vendor's own "yes sir rib" on record, absent from their own photographs.
  5. "Missed multiple times" needs precision. Only dates 2 and 4 (20 & 27 Jul) were affirmative vendor commitments in words; dates 1 and 3 were job-card entries / an indefinite deferral. State two express commitments plus two silent slippages.
